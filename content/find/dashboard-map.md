## How the screen is laid out

| Area | What lives there |
|---|---|
| Top bar | Search, connection status, feature requests, notifications |
| Left sidebar | The pages: Sessions, Schedule, Artifacts, apps, Customize, Settings |
| Session list | Your chats, plus New and the menus for many chats at once |
| The chat | The conversation, and the side panel when you open it |
| Message box | What you type, plus attachments, goals and the approval mode |
| Under the message box | This chat's agent, project folder, context and model |

The last row is the one people miss. Every chat has its own agent, project folder and model, and you change all three right there, under the message box.
